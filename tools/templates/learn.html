Crochet Academy

Beginner Guides

Start with a slip knot and a chain stitch. Practise single crochet in rows until your edges are straight, then try a granny square. Use a smooth cotton yarn and a 4 to 5 mm hook for your first project.

Care and Maintenance

Hand wash in cool water with a gentle soap, press out the water without wringing, and dry flat. Store folded, never on a hanger, so the stitches keep their shape.

Styling Ideas

Layer a crochet halter under an open shirt, pair a cover-up with a plain swimsuit, or carry a granny-square tote to lift a simple outfit.
